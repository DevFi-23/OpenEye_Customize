## 1. What the two commits change

**`9498271` "Added new parameters"** (Python + `regmap.yaml`):
* new configuration parameters `CYCLE_WIDTH`, `LIMIT_SIZE_IACT/WGHT/PSUM` (written into `parameters_FPGA.vh`);
* wider status fields: `needed_cycles` 18 -> 32 bit, `iact_glb_writing_cycles` 16 -> 24, `needed_iact_cycles_reg` 4 -> 6,
  `fsm_psum_limit` 17 -> 24, iact read/write limits `LIMIT_SIZE_IACT` bit, iact increments and `iact_buffer_words_per_write`
  / `needed_iact_buffer_words` `BUFFER_WIDTH_IACT + 5 - log2(CLUSTERS)` bit (16 at 2 clusters), psum address increments
  `BUFFER_WIDTH_PSUM` bit; the unused field `wght_cycles_reg` removed.

**`36395dd` "Implemented more functional layers for bigger inputs and single channel"** (RTL, Python, testbench):
* `OpenEye_FPGA.v`, `OpenEye_Parallel.v`, `psum_pipeline.v`: counters and ports `CYCLE_WIDTH` / `BUFFER_WIDTH_*` wide
  (`fsm_cycle`, `current_cycle`, `finished_cycles_*`, `fsm_psum_cycle`, `needed_cycles`, psum address increments), unused
  ports `wght_cycles_reg`, `iact_needed_cycles` removed; the weight-send hold `fsm_sending_cycle <= fsm_sending_cycle`
  moved before the enable block (still after the increment, so no change of behaviour);
* **single channel:** `iact_stream_constructor.v` drops every special path for one input channel (the row-pair read
  addresses `single_read_half` / `single_output_half`, the write address `single_write_row/column`); `data_pipeline_iact.v`
  (inside every PE) wraps its sub-word counter after every sub-word when `first_spad_max_i == 1` (one channel per PE) and
  drops the "uneven ending" bookkeeping that existed for that case;
* Python: the DAG data layout of `c342d04` (every `dram.fmap[...]` gets one more index for layers with several inputs, new
  `input` / `add` layers in `DRAM.py`), `iact_read_inc_2 = ceil(iact_buffer_words_per_write / diff_iact_layer)`, default
  quantization shift of a layer without factors 7 -> 6, `data_create` model edits; `test_dense_output.py` adapted to the
  DAG index.

## 3. Assessment

* **New in upstream and useful for us:** #17 fixed in the RTL (one-channel images wider than one x pass), so the Python
  padding of `channel_pad.py` could go and a one-channel first layer would take about 5 % fewer cycles; #37 fixed by
  wider fields (and room for bigger networks: 32-bit cycle counters).
* **Not mergeable as is:** the Python side depends on the DAG rewrite (`c342d04`, not taken: conflicts with our DRAM model,
  profiler hooks and quantization path); the constructor of upstream is the `551cf85` line (`dev` kept its own, with the
  fixes of #6, #7, #13, #19), and upstream still has #29, #36 and fails Dense 3136 -> 10, all right in `dev`. A merge would
  bring those back.
* **Recommendation:** do not merge the commits; port two parts by hand, each as an RTL change for approval with the full
  verification:
  1. **#37**: the wider iact fields of `9498271` (regmap + the matching RTL wires), adapted to our `regmap.yaml`; check that
     the status word count stays 28 / 14 / 7.
  2. **#17**: the one-channel rework of `36395dd` (`data_pipeline_iact.v` sub-word wrap, the special paths of
     `iact_stream_constructor.v` removed) on top of our constructor, then `channel_pad` off: the channel sweep of
     `test_mnist_layers` (1/2/3/4/8 channels x `NUM_GLB_IACT` 1, 3), the MNIST conv tests, the trained models, the full
     regression and the baselines (MNIST conv1 cycles expected to drop).
