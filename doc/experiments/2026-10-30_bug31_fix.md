# 2026-10-30: bug #31 fixed in PE.v (fix A, approved by the user)

Bug #31 (`2026-10-30_bug31_empty_pass.md`): a compute pass in which no PE holds a non-zero activation stops every later
pass of the layer (outputs = bias + earlier passes). Bugs #33 (Dense) and #35 (conv3 of the real model,
`2026-10-30_bug32_bias_order.md`) are the same bug. The user approved fix A: the change stays inside the PE.

## 1. The pass protocol and what was missing

Per pass:
1. `OpenEye_Parallel` sends `compute_i` to every used PE at once (COMPUTING: `psum_transmitted` and no iact / weight
   transfer for a few clocks).
2. A PE with data leaves IDLE (`PE.v`, IDLE) and drops its iact ready; the cluster ready is the AND of its PEs
   (`PE_cluster.v`), so the top-level bus `iact_ready_o_oep_w` leaves all-ones.
3. `OpenEye_FPGA` Process 2 counts the pass (`current_cycle`); Process 5 starts the converter and the weight send of the
   next pass.
4. The PE computes, raises its ready (the next pass's data are loaded) and waits in WAIT_TO_SEND_PSUM.
5. The psum FSM (`psum_pipeline.v`) waits in WAIT_TO_SEND_READY_SIGNAL for 16 clocks without transfer, feeds the psums
   in (CALCULATE_PSUM, once every chain start is ready), reads them out (PSUM_GET_RESULTS, the PEs go to SEND_PSUM and
   IDLE) and returns to WAIT_TO_SEND_READY_SIGNAL, which sets `psum_transmitted` again: step 1 for the next pass.

A PE without a non-zero activation (`second_spad_words_iact == 0`) or weight word stayed in IDLE. `compute_pe` still
cleared its data pipelines (the data side was consistent), but when no PE of a pass had data, step 2 did not happen,
so neither did step 3: the next pass was never loaded, while steps 1 and 5 went on (an IDLE PE passes psums through in
SEND_PSUM), so the layer ended on time with only the earlier passes.

## 2. The change (`hdl/PE.v`, both FSMs: sparse, used by the platform, and dense)

New state `EMPTY_PASS` (9). In IDLE, `data_set & compute_pe` with no activation or no weight word now goes to
EMPTY_PASS instead of staying: iact and weight ready low (the pass is counted and the next one is loaded, as for a
computing PE), `psum_select = 1` (ready for psums at once). In EMPTY_PASS the ready stays low for `EMPTY_PASS_HOLD` = 16
clocks, or until the psum FSM reads the pass out (`psum_enable_i`) if that comes first. On `psum_enable_i` the PE goes to
SEND_PSUM exactly as from IDLE (`adder_en = 1`, `use_psum = 0`, `used_psum_memory = 0`). No SPad is read and no product
is formed; SEND_PSUM passes the incoming psum on unchanged, which is what an idle PE has always done in a pass with data
elsewhere. Cost: one state, a 5-bit counter per PE.

PEs that never received data in the layer (`data_set = 0`) or get no `compute_i` (`compute_mask`) stay in IDLE as before.
A PE with data takes the unchanged path.

### How long the ready has to stay low (two versions rejected)

| Version | Conv cases | Dense 3136 -> 10 with a zero block | Cycles of the 12 baseline cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| ready back after 6 clocks (the shortest computing pass) | right | **hang** | - |
| ready low until the read-out (`psum_enable_i`) | right | right | **+5 to +11 % compute** in small convs, +3.9 % `model_mnist_real` |
| **ready low 16 clocks or until the read-out** (final) | right | right | **0 cycles, 0 DMA words** on all 12 |

Why 6 clocks hung (traced with `probe32.py` `B31_TOP` / `B31_WATCH`): the next pass's weights and activations were then
transferred while the psum FSM was still in WAIT_TO_SEND_READY_SIGNAL (`psum_transmitted` = 1; it leaves after 16 clocks
without transfer). When the transfer ended, OpenEye_Parallel (COMPUTING: `psum_transmitted` and no transfer) sent a
second compute pulse, one pass early, before the empty pass had been read out. The PEs held it as pending
(`compute_pending`) and started the next pass as soon as they were back in IDLE, in one pass on 4 of its 12 activation
words; the passes went out of step and a PE hung in CALCULATING. In a normal pass the transfer starts after the PEs have
computed (more than 16 clocks), when the psum FSM is in CALCULATE_PSUM / PSUM_GET_RESULTS (`psum_transmitted` = 0), so it
cannot trigger a compute pulse. (A real pass shorter than 16 clocks in every PE would meet the same race; not seen.)

Why "until the read-out" cost cycles: many passes with data also have empty PEs (the PEs beyond the end of a line, e.g.
x = 28..31 when a 28-pixel line is mapped on 32 PE columns). The cluster ready is the AND of its PEs, so these PEs delayed
the transfer of the next pass for their cluster from "the computing PEs are done" to "the read-out has started".

16 clocks: the PE starts after the compute pulse, which OpenEye_Parallel sends after the psum FSM has entered
WAIT_TO_SEND_READY_SIGNAL; while the ready is low no transfer can start, so the psum FSM counts its 16 idle clocks and
leaves that state before the empty PE releases the ready. In a pass with data the computing PEs hold the bus longer than
16 clocks, so the empty PEs change nothing.

## 4. Tools

* `OPENEYE_ZERO_BLOCK=y:<a>:<b>` / `k:<a>:<b>` (testbench): zero a block of the layer-0 input (the older
  `OPENEYE_ZERO_IACTS` zeroes all of it, where losing later passes changes nothing).
* `2026-10-30_bug32/probe32.py`: `B31_TOP=<t0>:<t1>` logs the pass control (main / psum FSM, `psum_transmitted`,
  OpenEye_Parallel FSM and `finished_cycles`, compute pulses, iact ready / enable, weight enable, `current_cycle`).
* `2026-10-30_bug31/run31.py` cases as in `2026-10-30_bug31_empty_pass.md`.
